
# HR Employee Attrition Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes employee attrition to identify patterns and factors associated with employees leaving an organization.

The analysis is based on an HR dataset containing 1,470 employees and 35 columns.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- SQL Server
- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX

## Project Objectives
- Calculate the overall employee attrition rate.
- Analyze attrition across departments and job roles.
- Identify attrition patterns by age, income, and tenure.
- Explore the relationship between workplace factors and attrition.
- Present findings through interactive dashboards.

## Project Process

### 1. Data Preparation
- Reviewed and prepared the HR dataset.
- Checked data quality and missing values.
- Organized data for analysis.

### 2. SQL Analysis
- Used SQL queries to explore and analyze employee data.
- Applied filtering, aggregation, joins, and conditional logic.
- Validated the dataset.

### 3. Power BI Dashboard
Created a two-page interactive dashboard:

**Page 1: HR Overview**
- Total Employees
- Employees Left
- Overall Attrition Rate
- Attrition by Job Role
- Attrition by Department
- Attrition by Age Group
- Attrition by Income Band
- Attrition by Tenure
- Attrition by Marital Status

**Page 2: Workplace Factors**
- Attrition by Overtime
- Attrition by Job Satisfaction
- Attrition by Environment Satisfaction
- Attrition by Job Involvement
- Attrition by Number of Companies Worked
- Attrition by Job Level

## Key Findings
- Total Employees: 1,470
- Employees Left: 237
- Overall Attrition Rate: 16.12%
- Sales Department Attrition Rate: 20.63%
- Sales Representative Attrition Rate: 39.76%
- Employees under 25 had an attrition rate of 39.18%.
- Employees with 0–1 year of tenure had an attrition rate of 34.88%.
- Employees working overtime had a higher attrition rate (30.53%) compared with those who did not (10.44%).

## Business Recommendations
- Review employee retention strategies in the Sales Department.
- Improve onboarding and support for new employees.
- Investigate workload and overtime practices.
- Review employee satisfaction and workplace conditions.
- Monitor attrition rates across different job roles and employee groups.

## Important Analytical Note
The findings show associations between employee characteristics and attrition. They do not necessarily indicate direct causation.
